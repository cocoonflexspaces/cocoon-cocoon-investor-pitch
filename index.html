Imagine the city of the future.
Cocoon is a laboratory
for the city
of the future.
Tech, health, transportation — all radically transformed. Our cities? Barely. The reason: real estate is controlled by the most risk-averse investors in the world. Small owners and family offices don't take risks. No one has been able to unlock them — until now.
Cocoon changes the equation by bringing high cash flow into the least liquid layer of the city. That cash flow becomes the lever for something far bigger: a platform where top architects, urbanists, and minority creators can beta-test the city of the future — at every scale.
ERA COMMONS
The foundation arm built alongside Cocoon — grant-eligible, city-by-city, designed to scale with every market Cocoon enters. And engineered to become a marketing engine in each one.
Every landlord, family office, and developer we serve generates the margin — and the network of spaces and connections — that makes this possible. Many of them own assets in underserved communities too. The business model and the mission are the same engine.
Unlock the spaces
We monetize design-forward venues for risk-averse owners — then use that commercial trust to bring art, culture, design, and dignifying experiences to underserved communities. Access to significant spaces should not be a privilege of wealth.
Bring nature back to the city
Not biophilic plant walls. A radical, urbanistic reintroduction of nature into the city itself — green corridors, living infrastructure, and public spaces that challenge how cities are built at the planning level.
A platform for all creators
From top architects, designers, and urbanists to minority creators with social visions — Cocoon + Era Commons is where ideas meet venues. Micro to macro beta tests of what equitable, beautiful cities could look like.
Win-win for every party
Real estate owners get cash flow. Brands get access. Creators get venues. Communities get culture. Investors get a business model that generates social impact as a byproduct of profitability — not a cost of it.

Our Strategy
Four moves that compound.
Move 1
Ascend the property tier ladder
Private residences → family offices → institutional developers. Silverstein, Naftali, and Corcoran Sunshine are the beachhead. Higher-value properties unlock larger bookings.
→ Larger spaces = larger activations
Move 2
Live · Stage 1
AI-first operating system
Already in first stage with strong early results — increasing revenue per booking and reducing costs. Automates proposals, scheduling, and vendor coordination. Scales to 2× properties with the same team.
→ Leverage without headcount
Move 3
Scarcity by design + predictable income
Cocoon brand capped at 200 properties per city to protect the premium. Mid-tier scale launches under a separate marketplace brand — preserving exclusivity while capturing volume. The result: predictable, subscription-style income from boost placements, memberships, and access tiers layered on top of transactional revenue. Investors see recurring, forecastable cash flows — not just one-off bookings.
→ Recurring revenue layer
Move 4
Monetize the Discovery Portfolio
Institutional venues pay to belong to the private Discovery Portfolio. Event production companies pay for curated access. A private marketplace within the marketplace — its own revenue layer.
→ New B2B revenue stream
