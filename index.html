How We Work
Two journeys. One platform.
Property Owner Journey
1
Onboarding, photography & campaigns
We photograph, write listings, set pricing, and run marketing campaigns. Our Casa Manager creates fresh content after each booking.
2
Booking management
We handle all inquiries, vetting, contracts, and scheduling.
3
Event day + cleaning
Casa Manager on-site. Professional cleaning after every event. Owner is never involved.
4
Post-event & payment
Cleaning, damage check, payout. Owner receives monthly report.
Result: Passive income from a space they already own. No effort, no liability, no surprises.

Proposal & booking

3
Event day
Casa Manager manages the space. Client focuses on their event.
4
Repeat & refer
99% 5-star reviews. The majority of large bookings come from referrals.

Our Strategy
Four moves that compound.
Move 1
Ascend the property tier ladder
Private residences → family offices → institutional developers. Silverstein, Naftali, and Corcoran Sunshine are the beachhead. Higher-value properties unlock larger bookings.
→ Larger spaces = larger activations
Move 2
Live · Stage 1
AI-first operating system
Already in first stage with strong early results — increasing revenue per booking and reducing costs. Automates proposals, scheduling, and vendor coordination. Scales to 2× properties with the same team.
→ Leverage without headcount
Move 3
Scarcity by design + predictable income
Cap at 200 properties per city to preserve quality. Add recurring revenue: property boost placements, marketplace membership, and access subscriptions — predictable income on top of transactional.
→ Recurring revenue layer
Move 4
Monetize the Discovery Portfolio
Institutional venues pay to belong to the private Discovery Portfolio. Event production companies pay for curated access. A private marketplace within the marketplace — its own revenue layer.
→ New B2B revenue stream
